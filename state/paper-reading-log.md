# Paper Reading Log

> Starts being used in Phase 9+ (you'll read "Attention Is All You Need" after implementing
> attention) and becomes central in Phase 12 (research skills). Reading a paper means being able
> to state its question, assumptions, method, and limitations — and ideally reproduce a baseline.

## Reading method
1. **Pass 1 (5 min):** title, abstract, figures, conclusion. What's the claim?
2. **Pass 2 (~1 hr):** read fully, note assumptions and the core method. Skip heavy proofs.
3. **Pass 3 (deep):** re-derive key equations; try to reproduce a baseline or a figure.

## Log
### Template
```
## <Paper title> (<year>) — <authors / venue>
- Link:
- Research question:
- Key assumptions:
- Core method (in my words):
- Main result:
- Limitations / what could be wrong:
- Reproduced? (what I ran, what I got):
- Connections to what I've learned:
- Date read:
```
